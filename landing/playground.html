Feel the power of VeloxQuant-MLX

Dial in your Mac, model and goal — see the recommended compression method, how much more context fits in your RAM, and real measured benchmarks. Every number here is computed live from the same heuristics that ship in veloxquant recommend, or read from committed benchmark data. Nothing is faked.

Recommender + compression estimates are transparent heuristics (a direct port of veloxquant_mlx/tools/mac_recommender.py) — they state when resident RAM savings are unlikely. Benchmark charts read measured values committed under figures/. For live numbers on your own machine, run python -m veloxquant_mlx benchmark.
