# Contributors

People who have made a non-trivial merged contribution to VeloxQuant-MLX — a
bug fix, a new benchmark, a documentation improvement, a new quantization
method, and so on. Every merge is already credited via git/GitHub history and
[CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md); this list is the additional, standing record of
who's contributed. See [GOVERNANCE.md's Authorship & Acknowledgement
Policy](GOVERNANCE.md#authorship--acknowledgement-policy) for how a
contribution earns a listing here versus a paper acknowledgement or
co-authorship, which are reserved for a higher bar than an ordinary merge.

This file is maintained alongside merges; if you believe a contribution of
yours should be listed and isn't, open an issue or say so on the relevant PR.
